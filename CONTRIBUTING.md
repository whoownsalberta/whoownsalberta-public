# Contributing to WhoOwnsAlberta.info

We welcome independent review, factual corrections, primary-source pointers,
and open-government data importer proposals from journalists, researchers,
analysts, and Albertans.

## How to contribute

Use the GitHub Issue templates in this repository:

1. **Report a correction** — Spot an error, outdated status, missing
   counterevidence, or newer official ruling/audit affecting a published page?
   Open a **Correction** issue with the exact entry/page ID and primary source.
2. **Submit a primary-source tip** — Know of a public Order in Council,
   Auditor General report, court judgment, Elections Alberta filing, lobbyist
   registry record, or FOIP release that clarifies an open question? Open a
   **Primary-source tip** issue with the official URL and pinpoint locator.
3. **Propose a dataset importer** — Want to help build reproducible parsers for
   Alberta Open Government datasets (such as Blue Book payments, sole-source
   disclosures, or regulatory filings)? Open a **Dataset importer** issue.

## Evidence, privacy, and legal rules (required)

To protect contributors, readers, and private individuals:

- **Primary records first:** Link directly to official public records (statutes,
  Hansard, Auditor General reports, audited financial statements, Orders in
  Council, court rulings, official registries). Secondary news reporting is fine
  as a lead, not proof.
- **Do not upload copyrighted articles or bulk third-party PDFs:** Share the
  official URL, document title, date, and page/section locator instead.
- **No private personal information:** Never post home addresses, personal phone
  numbers or emails, medical/patient records, family details, or unredacted
  FOIP dumps containing private individuals' data.
- **No unsubstantiated accusations:** Distinguish what a document proves from
  allegations or hypotheses. Shared donors, appointments, or corporate parent
  listings are leads for verification, not proof of wrongdoing.

## How submissions are reviewed

All public issues and tips are treated as **unverified leads** until an
independent researcher and independent reviewer reopen the underlying official
records, check counterevidence and official accounts, assess source rights and
privacy/defamation risks, and record exact slice-level approvals in the private
editorial workflow. Accepted updates are compiled offline and published here
through a verified release PR with full provenance in `site/manifest.json`.
