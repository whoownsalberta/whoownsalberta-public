Absence of a documented formal business case (OAG finding)

Evidence status: documented.

The Auditor General found no evidence that AHS or Alberta Health developed a formal business case before initiating the procurement. This is an audit finding subject to the auditor’s stated indeterminate access limitation, not proof that no other analysis existed.
