Both Auditor General DynaLIFE recommendations remain Not Ready for Assessment in November 2026

Evidence status: documented.

One year after the November 2025 examination, both joint OAG recommendations (procurement analysis before major spending; records management documenting key decision-makers and rationale) remain listed as Not Ready for Assessment in the November 2026 status summaries for Hospital and Surgical Health Services (Nov. 16, 2026) and Primary and Preventative Health Services (November 2026 edition). On November 17, 2026, Auditor General Phil Peters testified at Public Accounts Committee that both remain outstanding. In March 18, 2026 Main Estimates, Minister Matt Jones testified that the health system is acting on all recommendations; Not Ready for Assessment means the OAG has not yet been notified that implementation is ready for audit verification, not that zero internal work has started.
