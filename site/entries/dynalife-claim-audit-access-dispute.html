Dispute over records access during the Auditor General examination

Evidence status: disputed. Disputed; accounts differ.

The Auditor General said AHS restricted access to records with Department of Health support and that the impact of missing or redacted material was indeterminate. Premier Smith told the Assembly the government fully cooperated and disputed suggestions otherwise. On November 17, 2026, Auditor General Phil Peters testified at Public Accounts Committee that AHS engaged the legal team reviewing DynaLIFE documents and that OAG is not seeing the same access dynamics in its ongoing AHS procurement audit, though legal privilege remains an issue. Available records do not establish personal intent behind the access limits.
