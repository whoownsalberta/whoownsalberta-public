2020-2026 enforcement, detention, and data statutes span both Kenney and Smith governments plus a 2007 PC origin

Evidence status: documented.

Primary statutes show a multi-premier expansion of provincial enforcement and detention authorities: under Premier Jason Kenney, Bill 1 (2020) enacted the Critical Infrastructure Defence Act (CIDA); under Premier Danielle Smith, Bill 11 (2024) and Bill 49 (2025) created the independent agency police framework, Bill 31 (2024) and Bill 45 (2025) expanded CIDA to a two-kilometre U.S. border corridor and energy facilities, Bill 53 (2025) enacted the Compassionate Intervention Act authorizing involuntary addiction detention, Bill 4 (2025) expanded Clare's Law collection and disclosure of health and personal records for threat assessments, and Bill 15 (2026) authorized delegating Safer Communities and Neighbourhoods Act (originally enacted in 2007 under PC Premier Ed Stelmach) powers to ASPS.
