Alberta Next Panel advised against a policing referendum despite 52% polling opposition, and the October 19, 2026 ballot omitted RCMP replacement

Evidence status: documented.

In December 2025, the government-appointed Alberta Next Panel recommended transitioning community policing from the RCMP to an Alberta Police Service prior to the 2032 contract expiry without holding a referendum—arguing that over 80% of Albertans already receive policing from municipal services or Alberta Sheriffs—even though its own report recorded 52% opposition in professional polling (44%, 41%, and 48% support across three 2025 waves, lowest of seven measures) and majority opposition in online surveys. Cabinet's Orders in Council 109/2026, 110/2026, and 160/2026 set ten questions for the October 19, 2026 stand-alone referendum with zero questions on RCMP replacement.
