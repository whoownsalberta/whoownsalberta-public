April 16, 2026 Order in Council 116/2026 repealed the regulatory rule separating corporate directors from the Oversight Board

Evidence status: documented.

On July 2, 2025, Order in Council 241/2025 enacted AR 163/2025, whose section 3(3)(a) barred members of the Independent Agency Police Service Oversight Board from serving concurrently on the corporate board of directors. Nine months later, on April 16, 2026, Cabinet issued Order in Council 116/2026 (AR 72/2026) repealing section 3(3)(a) to remove that board-overlap bar—seven months before Bill 44 proposed abolishing the corporate structure altogether.
