Why some headlines report $109 million

Why some headlines report $109 million

Evidence status: attributed claim. Attributed claim.

November 19, 2025 Canadian Press reporting published by Global News reported roughly $109 million in taxpayer costs by adding the Auditor General’s $77 million extra-cost estimate to the $32 million rounded acquisition figure ($77M + $31.5M unrounded = $108.5M). The Auditor General’s 48-page November 2025 report never prints $109 million and excludes the $32 million acquisition from its unsuccessful-procurement cost table because AHS acquired operating assets and assumed liabilities at fair value. WhoOwnsAlberta.info keeps these accounting bases separate rather than combining them.
