Bill 44 repeals the Alberta Sheriffs Police Service's Crown-corporation status and moves staff under the Public Service Act

Evidence status: documented.

Introduced on November 23, 2026 under Premier Danielle Smith as a 163-page First Reading omnibus bill (168-page PDF amending 20 provincial statutes), Section 12 of Bill 44 repeals the Police Act's 2025 Crown-corporation structure (s. 33.4(2)) and the Bill 15 employee-transfer chapters (Parts 3.3 and 3.4). It establishes the Alberta Sheriffs Police Service directly as a non-corporate statutory force whose employees are appointed under the Public Service Act, shifts municipal service contracts under s. 33.75 to the Crown in right of Alberta, and provides a three-year transitional wind-down window for the existing corporation (s. 33.951).
