Voted Alberta Sheriffs operating budgets rose from $136.7M (2024-25) and $156.0M (original 2025-26) to $200.942M in 2026-27

Evidence status: documented.

Reconciling Alberta's 2025-26 and 2026-27 Government Estimates on a single voted-budget basis, Program 2.1 (renamed Alberta Sheriffs Police Service Funding) rose from $136.738M (2024-25 Budget) and $156.035M (original 2025-26 Estimate; restated to $164.066M Comparable Budget after an intra-ministry +$8.031M reclassification from Law Enforcement Oversight alongside splitting out the Police Review Commission) to $200.942M in 2026-27, incorporating +$36.9M for ASPS implementation. Program 2.4 Contract Policing and Policing Oversight rose from $397.192M to $432.289M, bringing the combined two-program Estimates total from $553.227M (original 2025-26; $561.258M restated) to $633.231M. Separate 2026-27 PSES Business Plan allocations include $14.9M for border Sheriffs Interdiction Patrol Teams and $26.4M for the Police Review Commission.
