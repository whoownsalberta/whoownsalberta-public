DynaLIFE community lab contract — overview

Public-record overview of how Alberta directed, contracted, and reversed expanded community lab outsourcing.

Decision and countercontext

The Auditor General examined how Alberta Health and AHS proceeded with expanded community laboratory outsourcing between 2019 and 2023, alongside the compliant written RFP finding and government rationale.

Ministerial and department strategic direction (OAG finding)

Evidence status: documented.

The Auditor General found that the then health minister and Department of Health expected the 2019–22 community laboratory outsourcing to proceed, while AHS management and board were not the strategic decision-makers. This finding does not attribute initiation to Danielle Smith.

Written RFP met standards; Sonic withdrew citing pandemic travel limits

Evidence status: documented.

The Auditor General found the written RFP met applicable document standards. After Sonic Healthcare withdrew citing pandemic travel restrictions, an independent procurement adviser said AHS could negotiate with the remaining qualified proponent. This did not resolve the auditor’s separate concerns about proceeding with one bidder.

AHS and Premier Smith rationale for outsourcing and reversal

Evidence status: attributed claim. Attributed claim.

AHS described improving service delivery as the purpose of bringing DynaLIFE operations under APL in 2023. The Auditor General reports that savings were the primary earlier objective of outsourcing. Premier Smith later said her government reversed course after seeing DynaLIFE could not manage provincewide service.

Separate money measures

Public records report three distinct financial measures that answer different questions and must not be summed: a cancelled $4.8 billion prospective commitment, $77 million in auditor-estimated extra costs, and $31.5 million in disclosed cash purchase consideration.

$4.8 billion estimated initial commitment (cancelled August 31, 2023)

Evidence status: documented.

AHS reported executing the services and ancillary agreements on May 30, 2022, with an estimated $4.8 billion commitment over an initial 14 years and four months. The related commitment ended on August 31, 2023. This was a prospective contract estimate, not cash paid.

$77 million auditor-estimated extra cost for 2019–23 initiative

Evidence status: documented.

The Auditor General estimated about $61 million in incremental cost against an estimated continuation of the prior service model over roughly eight months, plus $16 million to reintegrate services, producing $77 million for the 2019–23 initiative. These are audit-assessed additional costs, not gross payments to DynaLIFE.

$31.5 million disclosed cash purchase consideration ($29.388M net cash outflow)

Evidence status: documented.

AHS’s audited statements report $31.5 million cash purchase consideration for acquiring DynaLIFE operations on August 31, 2023, after settling $16.295 million in balances due from DynaLIFE. The same transaction appears as $29.388 million net cash outflow after cash acquired. The legal cash payee and owner-level distribution are unverified.
