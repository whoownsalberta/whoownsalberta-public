Money trail — commitment, audit cost estimate, and acquisition cash

Separate accounting bases for the $4.8B commitment, $77M extra-cost estimate, and $31.5M acquisition cash.

Disclosed public-record amounts

Each figure below stays on its source basis. Do not combine prospective contract commitments, counterfactual cost estimates, and asset-acquisition cash into a single purported loss.

$4.8 billion estimated initial commitment (cancelled August 31, 2023)

Evidence status: documented.

AHS reported executing the services and ancillary agreements on May 30, 2022, with an estimated $4.8 billion commitment over an initial 14 years and four months. The related commitment ended on August 31, 2023. This was a prospective contract estimate, not cash paid.

$77 million auditor-estimated extra cost for 2019–23 initiative

Evidence status: documented.

The Auditor General estimated about $61 million in incremental cost against an estimated continuation of the prior service model over roughly eight months, plus $16 million to reintegrate services, producing $77 million for the 2019–23 initiative. These are audit-assessed additional costs, not gross payments to DynaLIFE.

$31.5 million disclosed cash purchase consideration ($29.388M net cash outflow)

Evidence status: documented.

AHS’s audited statements report $31.5 million cash purchase consideration for acquiring DynaLIFE operations on August 31, 2023, after settling $16.295 million in balances due from DynaLIFE. The same transaction appears as $29.388 million net cash outflow after cash acquired. The legal cash payee and owner-level distribution are unverified.
